AI Interview

Stuck? Let the AI ask the questions. A warm, guided conversation pulls out the details and feelings that make your chapter come alive — then drops your answers straight in.

Voice to Story

Rather speak than type? Narrate your memories out loud and your words are transcribed automatically. Your voice, in your words — no rewriting required.

Memory Connections

Writing about your career and remember something that belongs elsewhere? Pin it. Your writing assistant quietly adds a relevant note to the right chapter without touching anything you wrote.

Draft A & Draft B

Each chapter can have two drafts, in a style you choose: close to your own words, or polished. Compare them side by side, edit them, and pick your favorite. Nothing you replace is ever lost.

Vault Privacy

Mark any chapter Private. It stays preserved for when you're ready — but stays completely hidden from the book you share with your family.

Your Autobiography

When you're ready, one click weaves all your chapters into a beautifully written first-person autobiography — ready to print, share, or keep forever.

How it works.

01

Choose a chapter

Pick any of the twelve chapters that feels right today. There's no order, no pressure, and nothing needs to be finished.

02

Write, talk, or let AI guide you

Type freely, record your voice, or tap AI Interview and just answer questions. All three paths lead to the same place — your story, in your words.

03

Draft and print

When you're ready, write a draft of any chapter. Compare two versions and choose one. Your book builds itself as you go. Print it or share it as a web page — a letter your kids will read for the rest of their lives.
